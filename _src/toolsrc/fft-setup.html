The relationships

Δf = Fmax / lines    Tblock = 1 / Δf = lines / Fmax
fs = 2.56 · Fmax    N = 2.56 · lines
Ttotal = Tblock · (1 + (navg − 1)(1 − overlap))

The 2.56 factor gives the anti-aliasing filter room to roll off above Fmax, and it's why analysers sample at 2.56× rather than the Nyquist minimum of 2×.

Resolution is set purely by how long you record. To separate two close peaks you need the bin spacing, widened by the window's effective noise bandwidth, to be several times smaller than their difference. A common rule of thumb is a gap of at least 4 bins with a Hann window. Zoom FFT or a lower Fmax with more lines both achieve this.
