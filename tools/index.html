Software package Under development

Skipper Vibration Analysis Suite

A complete offline vibration analysis package for Windows: everything these calculators do one at a time, plus full 3D modelling, in one workflow from raw survey data to finished report. Your data never leaves your machine.

  • ScreeningSpectral peaks and overall levels against Energy Institute main-line criteria, ISO 20816-3 zones and your own limits, with spectra shown exactly as measured. EI 2008 US 7,430,914 ISO 20816-3
  • Spectrum processingWindow-corrected amplitudes for tones and broadband signals, and interpolated peak frequencies between bins. Harris Gade–Herlufsen Grandke
  • Machine diagnosticsPeaks named from each machine's forcing frequencies, fault patterns at every bearing, and trends forecasting when zones C and D will be reached. Randall ISO 20816-3
  • 3D models & FE modalPiping, steel and machine trains in 3D. Shear-deformable (Timoshenko) frame elements, B31J flexibility factors and CAESAR II-style restraints, solved by subspace iteration. Przemieniecki Cowper Bathe ASME B31J CAESAR II
  • ODS & modal testOperating deflection shapes on the model, and roving-hammer test modes paired with FE modes through a MAC table. Randall
  • RotordynamicsLateral critical speeds, unbalance response and stability to API 617, API 610 and API 684. Benchmarked against ROSS: frequencies agree to 1 part in a million. Friswell API 617 API 610 API RP 684 ROSS
  • PulsationAPI 618-style acoustic analysis of compressor and pump systems; shaking forces passed straight into the FE model. API 618 Blevins
  • FIV & AIVLikelihood-of-failure assessment to the Energy Institute method, verified against published worked examples. EI 2008 Carucci–Mueller Eisinger AFT example Bachoo 2021 GP&LNG 2020
  • Stress & fatigueVibration stress against ASME OM-3; rainflow and spectral fatigue (Dirlik, Benasciutti–Tovo, Zhao–Baker) cross-checked against FLife; DNV, BS 7608 and IIW S-N curves. ASME OM-3 ASTM E1049 Dirlik Benasciutti–Tovo FLife DNV-RP-C203 BS 7608 IIW
  • ReportsOne-click Word, PDF, PowerPoint and Excel reports, with the references behind every method used.

Validation: every calculation is checked against hand calculations, textbook cases or independent software, typically agreeing within 0.1%, and re-run by an automated test suite on every build.
