Vibration calculators

Engineering calculators that run in your browser. No install, no sign-up, and nothing you type or load is sent anywhere. Each one shows the formula it uses so you can check it.

Software package Under development

Skipper Vibration Analysis Suite

A complete offline package for vibration screening and analysis: everything these calculators do one at a time, in one workflow from raw survey data to finished report.

  • Import survey and analyser data, with spectra and waveforms shown exactly as measured
  • Screen measurements against user-defined criteria and standards
  • Compare before/after surveys to prove a fix worked
  • Report in one click to Word or PDF
  • Projects saved as single files you can share
  • Offline: runs on Windows with no install rights needed, and your data stays on your machine

Signal analysis

New · Analyser

FFT spectrum analyser

Load a CSV time signal and get a windowed, averaged spectrum with peak list, overall RMS and CSV export.

 New · Planning

FFT setup planner

Fmax, lines and averages to sample rate, resolution and acquisition time, with a separation check.

 Conversion

Vibration unit converter

Acceleration, velocity and displacement at any frequency, in peak, RMS or peak-to-peak.

 New · Conversion

Damping converter

Damping ratio, Q, loss factor, log decrement and half-power bandwidth: enter one, get the rest.

Machinery diagnostics

Bearings

Bearing defect frequencies

BPFO, BPFI, BSF and FTF from bearing geometry and shaft speed, with harmonics.

 New · Gears & belts

Gear mesh & belt frequencies

GMF, shaft speeds, hunting tooth and assembly phase frequencies, and belt frequency.

Structural dynamics

SDOF

SDOF natural frequency & transmissibility

Natural frequency from stiffness and mass, and the response to a forcing frequency, with a live plot.

 Piping / beams

Pipe span natural frequency

First four bending modes of a pipe or beam span, with contents and insulation, plus animated mode shapes.

 New · Panels

Plate natural frequency

Modes of a rectangular panel (duct walls, enclosures) with mode-shape maps and an excitation check.

 Random

Miles' equation

RMS response of an SDOF system to flat random base excitation.

Piping & flow

New · AIV

AIV sound power level

Carucci–Mueller sound power from a valve, PSV or orifice, with choked-flow check and sensitivity.

 New · Pulsation

Acoustic resonance & dead legs

Speed of sound, pipe and side-branch acoustic modes, and the flow velocities that can excite them.

 FIV

Vortex shedding frequency

Strouhal shedding frequency for a cylinder in flow, compared against a structure's natural frequency.
