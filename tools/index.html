Vibration calculators

Engineering calculators that run in your browser. No install, no sign-up, and nothing you type or load is sent anywhere. Every tool documents its method, assumptions and limits, how it was verified, and the sources behind it, linked to the Resources page.

Software package Under development

Skipper Vibration Analysis Suite

A complete offline package for vibration screening and analysis: everything these calculators do one at a time, in one workflow from raw survey data to finished report.

  • Import survey and analyser data, with spectra and waveforms shown exactly as measured
  • Screen measurements against user-defined criteria and standards
  • Compare before/after surveys to prove a fix worked
  • Report in one click to Word or PDF
  • Projects saved as single files you can share
  • Offline: runs on Windows with no install rights needed, and your data stays on your machine

Signal analysis

FFT spectrum analyserLoad a CSV time signal and get a windowed, averaged spectrum with peak list, overall RMS and CSV export. FFT setup plannerFmax, lines and averages to sample rate, resolution and acquisition time, with a separation check. Vibration unit converterAcceleration, velocity and displacement at any frequency, in peak, RMS or peak-to-peak. Damping converterDamping ratio, Q, loss factor, log decrement and half-power bandwidth: enter one, get the rest.

Machinery diagnostics

Bearing defect frequenciesBPFO, BPFI, BSF and FTF from bearing geometry and shaft speed, with harmonics. Gear mesh & belt frequenciesGMF, shaft speeds, hunting tooth and assembly phase frequencies, and belt frequency.

Structural dynamics

SDOF natural frequency & transmissibilityNatural frequency from stiffness and mass, and the response to a forcing frequency, with a live plot. Pipe span natural frequencyFirst four bending modes of a pipe or beam span, with contents and insulation, plus animated mode shapes. Plate natural frequencyModes of a rectangular panel (duct walls, enclosures) with mode-shape maps and an excitation check. Miles' equationRMS response of an SDOF system to flat random base excitation.

Piping & flow

AIV sound power levelCarucci–Mueller sound power from a valve, PSV or orifice, with choked-flow check and sensitivity. Acoustic resonance & dead legsSpeed of sound, pipe and side-branch acoustic modes, and the flow velocities that can excite them. Vortex shedding frequencyStrouhal shedding frequency for a cylinder in flow, compared against a structure's natural frequency.

These are for quick checks and preliminary estimates. For design decisions, use your own verified methods and the applicable standards. Spotted a bug or want a calculator added? Message me on LinkedIn.
