Damping converter

Damping comes in five different dialects depending on whether you measured it from a bump test, a ring-down or a frequency response. Enter any one and get the rest.

Ring-down tip: if the amplitude drops from A0 to An over n cycles, δ = ln(A0/An) / n.

The relationships

Q = 1 / 2ζ    η = 2ζ (at resonance)    δ = 2πζ / √(1 − ζ²)
Δf−3dB = 2ζ·fn = fn / Q

The half-power (−3 dB) bandwidth method is what you'd use on a measured frequency response or impact test. The log decrement is for a free-decay (ring-down) time signal. Q tells you how much a resonance amplifies a force at its natural frequency. All of these assume light, viscous damping, which is a good model for most steel structures and piping.
