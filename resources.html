What I read and recommend

A short, opinionated list. Books for depth, standards for the day job, and explainers that made something click.

Piping vibration (FIV / AIV)

  • Energy Institute: Guidelines for the avoidance of vibration induced fatigue failure in process pipework 2nd ed., 2008 The industry's go-to screening method for FIV, AIV, pulsation and small-bore connections. If you assess piping vibration, you'll live in this.
  • R. D. Blevins: Flow-Induced Vibration book The classic text on vortex shedding, galloping and flow-excited structures. Heavy, but it's where the physics behind the screening rules lives.

Machinery diagnostics & condition monitoring

  • R. B. Randall: Vibration-based Condition Monitoring book The best single book on the signal processing behind machine diagnostics: envelope analysis, cepstrum, order tracking, bearing and gear faults.
  • ISO 20816-3 standard Evaluation zones for vibration of industrial machines, measured on non-rotating parts. The current replacement for ISO 10816-3.
  • ISO 18436-2 standard The competence requirements behind vibration analyst certification (Categories I–IV). A useful map of what a complete analyst should know.

Vibration theory

  • J. P. Den Hartog: Mechanical Vibrations book Old, cheap and still one of the clearest books on the subject. Den Hartog explains with physical intuition first and maths second.
  • S. S. Rao: Mechanical Vibrations book The standard university textbook: thorough, well-structured, lots of worked examples.

Signal processing, visually

  • 3Blue1Brown: But what is the Fourier Transform? A visual introduction video The "winding a signal around a circle" intuition. The best 20 minutes you can spend on the Fourier transform.
  • Jack Schaedler: Seeing circles, sines and signals interactive An interactive primer on sampling, aliasing and the DFT. A big inspiration for the explainers on this site.
  • Bartosz Ciechanowski interactive essays Not about vibration, but the gold standard for teaching physical systems with interactive figures.
