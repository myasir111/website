From school maths to the spectrum

Most of the maths behind a vibration spectrum, you have already met. Find the row you last studied, and the lessons pick up from there.

At school

Signals

Right-angled triangles: sin, cos and Pythagoras. Angles in degrees and radians. Lesson S1

Physics

Hooke’s law, F = kx, and Newton’s second law, F = ma. Waves: frequency, wavelength and speed. Lesson P1

Ages 16 to 18

Signals

Graphs of sine and cosine, the angle-sum rule, differentiating and integrating sin and cos. Lesson S1, Lesson S2

Physics

Simple harmonic motion. Standing waves on a string. Resonance, described in words. Lesson P1; lesson P4 coming

At university

Signals

Fourier series and its coefficients: Lesson S2. Odd and even symmetry: Lesson S3. Complex numbers and the discrete Fourier transform: Lesson S4. Windows: Lesson S5. The wavelet transform: Lesson S6. Modulation and Bessel functions: Lesson S7.

Physics

The second-order differential equation, damping ratio, forced response, the wave equation and plate bending modes. Lesson P1; lessons P2 to P5 coming

On site

Signals

Orders and 1X, 2X; looseness and clipping; setting lines and Fmax (FFT setup planner); leakage and the Hann correction; knocks and impacts; gearbox and motor sidebands (gear mesh calculator).

Physics

Natural frequency checks (SDOF, pipe span, plate); damping (converter); where to brace a vibrating panel; vortex shedding (calculator).

Physics lessons P2 to P5 are being written now. Each one will be linked here when it's ready.
