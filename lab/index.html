Lab

Build the maths yourself

The trigonometry you met at school, the calculus and Fourier series from university, and the spectrum on a vibration analyser are the same maths. These lessons join them up, one step at a time, with a board you can drag at every step.

Each lesson opens with a puzzle, lists the facts you already know, derives the answer line by line, works two examples on a calculator, and ends with three practice problems. Not sure where to start? See the maths map.
