About

Muhammad Yasir Ali Khan

Vibration, Dynamics & Noise engineer based in Abu Dhabi, UAE.

I work on vibration problems in oil and gas facilities: flow-induced vibration (FIV) and acoustic-induced vibration (AIV) in piping, and diagnostics and condition monitoring of rotating equipment. My project work has covered gas processing facilities across the UAE, Bahrain and Iraq.

I'm interested in the space between the maths and the plant floor: explaining signal processing and dynamics clearly enough that the people who maintain the machines can use it.
