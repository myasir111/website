Overview

I'm a vibration engineer in Wood's Vibration, Dynamics & Noise team in Abu Dhabi, with 4+ years across oil & gas, cement and power. My work covers the full loop: predict vibration risk at design stage, measure what's actually happening on site, model it in FEA, and fix it with supports, design changes or maintenance actions.

I've worked on onshore and offshore production facilities, sour gas assets, LPG recovery, cement plants and hydro power stations.

Outside the day job I build Skipper, a vibration analysis app, plus the calculators and explainers on this site, because most vibration theory is easier than it looks once you can see it move.

What I do

01

Piping vibration assessment

FIV and AIV screening to Energy Institute guidelines, small-bore connection studies, flow-induced turbulence, dead-leg pulsation, slug flow, valve transients, cavitation and flashing.

02

Field testing & measurement

Multi-channel vibration, dynamic strain and pressure surveys on piping, structures and machinery with DEWESoft, Benstone and NI systems.

03

Modal testing & ODS

Experimental modal analysis (impact testing) and operating deflection shapes in ME'Scope VES to find natural frequencies, mode shapes and resonance problems.

04

Finite element analysis

Modal, harmonic and static stress analysis in Ansys and Autodesk Nastran, validated against field measurements. Piping flexibility in Caesar II.

05

Machinery diagnostics

Condition monitoring and fault diagnosis of gas engines, reciprocating compressors, pumps, fans and gearboxes: spectra, waveforms, valve-train dynamics and pressure-volume analysis.

06

Mitigation & support design

Bespoke piping and SBC supports and bracing, designed in SOLIDWORKS / Creo with fabrication drawings and MTOs, and checked against fatigue criteria.

07

Root cause & reliability

RCA on piping and equipment failures, FMEA, RCM and maintenance strategy, asset management (ISO 55000).

08

Signal processing & software

FFT, order and time-waveform analysis, custom analysis tools in Python, MATLAB and LabVIEW.
