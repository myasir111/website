Overview

I'm a vibration engineer at Wood in Abu Dhabi with 4+ years across oil & gas and heavy industry. Most of my work sits where fluids, structures and machines meet: screening process pipework and small-bore connections for vibration-induced fatigue, measuring what's actually happening on site, and turning that into supports, damping or maintenance actions that fix the problem.

On the machinery side I diagnose gas engines, reciprocating compressors, pumps, gearboxes and fans using vibration, pressure-volume and modal (EMA/ODS) testing.

Outside the day job I build Skipper, a vibration analysis app, and write the explainers on this site, because most vibration theory is easier than it looks once you can see it move.
